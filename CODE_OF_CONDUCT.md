# Code of conduct

Akatsuki is a small project. We want it to be a place where anyone can report a bug, ask a question or send a patch without being treated badly.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person, and make it specific.
- Accept that a "no" on a proposal is a decision about the project, not about you.
- Respect people's privacy. Never post someone else's email, address or personal details.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or intimidation, in public or in private.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chat) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to **sami@samihindi.com**. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer will review every report and may respond with a warning, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces, depending on what happened.
